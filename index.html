About Punla

Our process

You sign up online, we confirm your slot and send site details, you show up for a short orientation, then spend the morning planting alongside our foresters and fellow students.

Our mission

We make it simple for students to take part in real reforestation work — turning a free Saturday into shade, cleaner air, and stronger watersheds for the next generation.

Our commitment

We plant native and endemic species only, track survival rates after planting, and return to sites for maintenance — because a seedling in the ground isn't the finish line.

Our core values

Native species first

We only plant trees suited to the local ecosystem, sourced from accredited nurseries.

Student-friendly

Clear instructions, safety briefings, and certificates ready before you leave the site.

Long-term care

We monitor and maintain planted sites for at least two years after planting day.

Frequently asked questions

Is it free to join?

Yes — sign-up, seedlings, tools, and transport within the site are all free. Bring your own water and snacks.

Do I get proof for community service hours?

Every volunteer receives a signed certificate of participation on-site, valid for NSTP, CAS, or org requirements.

What should I wear and bring?

Closed shoes, clothes you don't mind getting muddy, sun protection, and a reusable water bottle. Gloves are provided.

Can I join without an organization or school group?

Yes, individual sign-ups are welcome — many volunteers join solo or with a few friends.
